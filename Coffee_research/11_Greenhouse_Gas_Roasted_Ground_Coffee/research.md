## 1. Executive Summary & Research Scope
Environmental sustainability and carbon footprint metrics have become indispensable quality parameters in modern global food supply chains, directly governing international export compliance, green certifications, and corporate ESG procurement. In Thailand, Robusta coffee represents ~80% of national production, with Chumphon serving as the epicenter.

This study established the first empirical Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) inventory and Greenhouse Gas (GHG) emission profile for roasted and ground Robusta coffee produced by community enterprises in Chumphon. The study surveyed **180 Robusta coffee farming households** and representative processing enterprises.

---

## 2. Real-Life Farming Baseline & Empirical Cultivation Data (Chumphon Survey)
- **Planting Density:** Average **145 coffee trees per rai** (spacing approx. $3.3 \times 3.3\text{ m}$).
- **Tree Productive Lifespan:** **30 to 50 years** (with periodic rejuvenation pruning / ตัดทำสาว).
- **First Harvest:** 3 years after field planting.
- **Fresh Cherry Yield:** Average **9.0 kg of fresh cherries per tree per year** (equivalent to ~**1,305 kg fresh cherries / rai / year**).
- **Intercropping Agroforestry:** Robusta is universally intercropped with fruit trees, including durian, mangosteen, southern langsat (longkong), and Para rubber.
- **Irrigation:** 100% rainfed agriculture (relying on Chumphon’s high annual monsoon rainfall).
- **Fertilizer Regime:**
  - Farmers regularly apply **0.60 kg of chemical fertilizer per tree every 4 months** (approx. 1.8 kg/tree/year).
  - Formulas commonly applied: **46-0-0 (Urea)**, **15-15-15 (Complete NPK)**, and **13-13-21 (High-potassium finishing formula)**.

---

## 3. Real-Life Processing Protocols Monitored
- **Primary Processing (Depulping & Washing):**
  - Fresh cherries soaked in water overnight to soften pulp and ferment mucilage.
  - Wet depulping and washing.
- **Sun-Drying:**
  - Washed parchment beans sun-dried on open yards for **1 to 3 days** (skin-drying/conditioning stage of pre-dried parchment lots; fresh whole cherries require 15–20 days under sun or 7–10 days in solar domes to reach 10–12% moisture).
- **Roasting Protocol:**
  - Roasted in commercial batch drum roasters over **3.5-hour operational processing shifts** with burner flue gas temperatures at **240 °C – 275 °C** (bean core drop temperatures terminate at 183 °C–186 °C for 12–18 minutes per batch) using **Liquid Petroleum Gas (LPG)**.
- **Milling / Grinding:**
  - Roasted beans ground to fine particle sizes using electric industrial burr grinders.

## 4. Carbon Footprint Results & Life Cycle Breakdown
The Functional Unit (FU) of analysis was **1.0 kg of roasted coffee** and **1.0 kg of ground coffee**:

| Life Cycle Stage | GHG Emissions (Roasted Coffee) | GHG Emissions (Ground Coffee) | Percentage Contribution | Primary Emission Driver |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Agricultural Cultivation Phase** | **1.26 kg CO₂e** | **1.26 kg CO₂e** | **75.4% (Hotspot)** | Nitrous oxide ($N_2O$) from synthetic nitrogen fertilizer application; fossil fuel for weeding machines |
| **Roasting Phase** | **0.41 kg CO₂e** | **0.41 kg CO₂e** | **24.5%** | Combustion of Liquid Petroleum Gas (LPG) in roaster burners |

| **Total Carbon Footprint** | **1.67 kg CO₂e / kg** | **1.69 kg CO₂e / kg** | **100.0%** | — |

## 5. Emission Reduction Strategies & Quantitative Reduction Potential
The study identified two high-impact interventions to decarbonize Thai coffee production:

1. **Optimal Nitrogen Fertilizer Application (Agronomic Intervention):**
   - The primary emission hotspot is excess chemical nitrogen fertilizer applied by farmers, which undergoes soil nitrification and denitrification to emit nitrous oxide ($N_2O$, a potent greenhouse gas with a Global Warming Potential 298 times that of $CO_2$).
   - Adopting the soil-tested fertilizer guidelines recommended by the **Chumphon Horticultural Research Centre** eliminates excess application, reducing GHG emissions by **0.60 kg CO₂e per 1 kg of roasted coffee** (a **36% total reduction**).
2. **High-Efficiency Roasting Burners (Processing Intervention):**
   - Replacing conventional atmospheric gas burners with insulated, high-efficiency burners reduces roaster LPG consumption by **69%**.
   - This eliminates **0.11 kg CO₂e per 1 kg of roasted coffee**.
3. **Combined Decarbonization Impact:**
   - Implementing both measures reduces the net carbon footprint from **1.67 kg CO₂e/kg down to 0.96 kg CO₂e/kg**—an impressive **43.0% overall GHG reduction**, qualifying Chumphon coffee for international low-carbon product labels and premium eco-conscious markets.
