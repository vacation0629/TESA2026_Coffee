## 1. Executive Summary & Organizational Model
The **Ban Tham Sing Coffee Community Enterprise (วิสาหกิจชุมชนกลุ่มกาแฟบ้านถ้ำสิงห์)** in Mueang Chumphon District, Chumphon Province, is widely celebrated as one of Thailand’s most successful farmer-led agricultural value-chain enterprises. Founded in 2008 under the leadership of Mr. Nikom Silapasorn (คุณนิคม ศิลปศร), the group broke free from the traditional cycle of farmer debt and exploitation by local middlemen by establishing a fully integrated **"Upstream-to-Downstream" (ปลูก-แปรรูป-จำหน่าย)** business model.

The enterprise operates exclusively with Robusta coffee cultivated within Tham Sing Subdistrict, controlling every phase: propagation, agroforestry cultivation, selective harvesting, wet/dry primary processing, precision roasting, packaging, and commercial retail distribution (roasted beans, ground coffee, and 3-in-1 / 4-in-1 herbal coffee formulations).

## 2. Agronomic & Microclimate Environment of Ban Tham Sing
- **Geographic Setting:** Tham Sing Subdistrict, Mueang Chumphon District. Characterized by undulating limestone hill terrain and fertile basaltic/volcanic red clay soils.
- **Soil Characteristics:** Deep red clay loam with excellent drainage, rich organic matter, and soil pH **5.0 – 6.5** in topsoil.
- **Climatic Parameters:**
  - Annual ambient temperature: **17 °C – 37 °C** (annual average **~27 °C**).
  - Annual relative humidity: **~81%**.
  - High annual rainfall (> 1,800–2,000 mm) driven by dual monsoons (Southwest and Northeast).
- **Intercropping Agroforestry Architecture:**
  - Robusta is grown in **multi-strata intercropped polycultures** alongside durian, mangosteen, southern langsat (longkong), betel nut, and rubber trees.
  - *Benefits:* Fruit tree canopies provide partial shade (filtering intense tropical solar radiation), buffer microclimatic heat stress, reduce weed growth naturally (minimizing herbicide dependency), and diversify farmer seasonal cash flow.

## 3. Real-Life Production & Post-Harvest Quality Parameters
From Chapter 5 of the monograph, the operational quality protocols practiced at Ban Tham Sing include:

### A. Phenology & Harvesting Schedule:
- **Flowering:** Synchronized blooming occurs in **March – April** following first post-dry rains.
- **Maturation Period:** 9 to 11 months from bloom to cherry maturity.
- **Harvesting Window:** Peak harvest spans **November through February/March**.
- **Harvest Method:** Selective manual harvesting prioritizing ripe red cherries (ผลสุกแดง), followed by immediate water immersion flotation to separate low-density floaters (เมล็ดลอย / defective/hollow cherries) from dense ripe sinkers (เมล็ดจม).

### B. Drying, Hulling, Aging & Moisture Standards:
- **Drying Protocol:** Cherries are dried on clean, elevated cement courtyards or enclosed solar drying facilities for **15 to 20 days** (Dry Natural Process), regularly raked and turned to ensure uniform moisture dissipation.
- **Moisture Threshold:** Sun-drying continues until bean moisture content drops to **11.0% – 13.0%** (ensuring safe storage without mold growth or enzymatic spoiling).
- **Milling & Hulling:** Dry cherries (กะลา/เปลือกแห้ง) are hulled using mechanical hullers to yield raw green coffee (สารกาแฟ).
- **1-Year Green Bean Resting / Cellar Aging Protocol:** Before roasting, Ban Tham Sing holds raw green coffee beans in conditioned warehouse storage for **up to 1 full year**. This resting period allows harsh, volatile organic acids to mellow, stabilizes moisture equilibria throughout the bean lot, and significantly subdues typical Robusta astringency, producing a rounder, sweeter cup profile.

## 4. Industrial Roasting Protocols & Dynamic Quality Adjustments
Ban Tham Sing operates commercial drum roasters equipped with bottom cooling pans and high-velocity suction fans to rapidly halt thermal carryover cooking:

| Roast Level Profile | Target Drum Temperature | Sensory / Flavor Character | Real-Life Dynamic Roasting Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dark Roast (คั่วเข้ม)** | **186 °C** | Intense, bold, low acidity, dark chocolate, smokiness; ideal for iced espresso/traditional Thai milk coffee | Temperature adjusted dynamically: $\pm 2 - 3^\circ\text{C}$ based on **initial green bean moisture** |
| **Medium / Light Roast (คั่วกลาง / คั่วอ่อน)** | **183 °C** | Balanced body, caramel sweetness, subtle fruity acidity; ideal for drip and Americano | Lower temperature charge prevents bean surface scorching |

### Critical Operational Control Rules:
1. **Color as Primary Quality Indicator:** In real production, the roasting master monitors **bean surface color and expansion (Agtron/visual browning)** as the primary decision threshold; temperature and roasting time act as secondary indicators.
2. **Post-Roast Hand Sorting:** Immediately after cooling, roasted whole beans are spread onto sorting tables where trained staff manually hand-pick any remaining defective, unexpanded, over-scorched, or discolored beans prior to packaging.
3. **Packaging Standard:** Roasted whole beans and ground coffee are nitrogen-flushed or packed into triple-laminated foil valve bags in standardized **0.5 kg (500 g)** units to prevent lipid oxidation and staleness.

## 5. Economic & Enterprise Governance Success Factors
- **Price Security for Member Farmers:** Ban Tham Sing guarantees purchase of member cherries at **substantially higher-than-market farmgate prices ($\ge 30\text{ THB/kg}$ cherry premium for 100% ripe red cherries**, more than double the ~12–15 THB/kg commodity rate), provided the cherries meet strict ripeness (0 green cherries) and density flotation standards.
- **Value Multiplication:**
  - Raw unhulled coffee commodity price: ~50–70 THB/kg.
  - Roasted and packaged specialty ground coffee: **350–500 THB/kg** (a 5x to 7x value multiplication retained within the community).
- **Dividend Distribution:** Net enterprise profits are distributed back to community members as annual dividends, community welfare funds, and agricultural development capital.
