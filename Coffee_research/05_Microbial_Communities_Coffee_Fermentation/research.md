## 1. Executive Summary & Context
Natural fermentation is a pivotal stage in coffee post-harvest processing. While traditionally viewed merely as a biological means to degrade the sticky pectinaceous mucilage layer surrounding the parchment coffee bean, fermentation is now recognized as the master biological engine responsible for generating volatile aromatic precursor compounds and defining specialty cup score.

In Thailand (producing ~18,689 tons of coffee annually across 387.94 km², with Arabica in Northern mountainous zones like Chiang Mai/Chiang Rai and Robusta dominating Southern provinces like Chumphon and Ranong), wet process fermentation is increasingly used to produce high-value specialty grades. This study performed deep metagenomic sequencing (16S rRNA gene sequencing for bacteria and ITS amplicon sequencing for fungi/yeast) alongside GC-MS metabolomics on on-site wet fermentation batches in Thailand to map the exact relationship between microbial community succession and aromatic/sensory metabolites.

---

## 2. Experimental Setup & High-Throughput Metagenomics
- **Process Monitored:** Real-life on-site wet process fermentation monitored from Day 0 through Day 8 (cherries and fermentation slurry).
- **Taxonomic Profiling:**
  - Bacterial 16S rRNA (V3-V4 hypervariable region) on Illumina platform.
  - Fungal/Yeast ITS1/ITS2 regions.
- **Metabolite & Volatile Profiling:** Gas Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry (GC-MS) coupled with chemometric multivariate correlation analysis (PCA, PLS-DA) connecting specific microbial taxa to volatile aroma compounds.

---

## 3. Microbial Diversity & Community Succession
A total of **64 bacterial genera** and **59 fungal/yeast genera** were identified actively participating in the fermentation consortium:

### A. Succession Dynamics (Bacteria vs. Yeast Timeline):
- **Initial Phase (Day 0–2):** Bacterial communities initiate the fermentation faster than yeasts/fungi, consuming initial monosaccharides (glucose, fructose) and driving rapid acidification.
- **Mid-to-Late Phase (Day 4–8):** Yeasts proliferate, metabolizing complex carbohydrates, producing pectinases, and synthesizing higher alcohols, esters, and terpene derivatives.

### B. Dominant Bacterial Taxa:
- **Lactic Acid Bacteria (LAB):**
  - *Lactococcus lactis*
  - *Leuconostoc citreum-holzapfelii*
  - *Leuconostoc pseudomesenteroides*
  - *Weissella* spp.
  - *Lactobacillus* spp.
- **Other Functional Bacteria:**
  - *Pseudomonas rhodesiae*
  - *Acinetobacter baylyi*
  - *Enterobacter* spp., *Novosphingobium* spp., *Gluconobacter* / *Acetobacter* (Acetic Acid Bacteria).

### C. Dominant Yeast & Fungal Taxa:
- *Pichia fermentans-kluyveri* (peaked strongly around **Day 4** of fermentation, then transitioned).
- *Saccharomyces cerevisiae*
- *Hanseniaspora uvarum* (well-known aroma enhancer in coffee and cocoa).
- *Wickerhamomyces anomalus*
- *Candida parapsilosis*
- *Papiliotrema flavescens*, *Papiliotrema laurentii*, *Papiliotrema terrestris*
- *Kurtzmaniella quercitrusa*, *Cladosporium* spp., *Rhodotorula mucilaginosa*.

## 4. Specific Correlations: Microbes to Aroma & Flavor Metabolites
Metagenomic correlation revealed that yeasts and lactic acid bacteria directly synthesized and modulated key volatile compounds governing sensory perception:

| Volatile Aroma Compound | Sensory Flavor Descriptor | Associated Microbial Taxa / Correlated Species |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Linalool** | Floral, citrus, sweet bois de rose | *Hanseniaspora uvarum*, *Aureobasidium thailandense*, *Rhodotorula mucilaginosa* |
| **Linalyl Formate** | Fruity, citrus, bergamot, floral | *Lactococcus lactis*, *Leuconostoc pseudomesenteroides* |
| **cis-Isoeugenol** | Sweet spicy, warm clove, balsamic | Predominant LAB consortia |
| **trans-Geraniol** | Rose-like, geranium, floral | Fermentation yeasts (*Pichia*, *Saccharomyces*) |
| **(-)-Isopulegol** | Minty, cool, fresh woody | Co-dominant yeast-bacterial interactions |
| **Ketones & Esters (e.g., Ethyl Acetate, Isoamyl Acetate)** | Fruity (apple, cherry, banana), winey | *Pichia fermentans*, *Hanseniaspora*, *Candida parapsilosis* |

## 5. Real-Life Quality Parameters & Operational Controls
1. **Pectinase Enzymatic Digestion:**
   - Yeasts (*Saccharomyces cerevisiae*, *Pichia kudriavzevii*, *Wickerhamomyces anomalus*, *Papiliotrema flavescens*) secrete endo- and exo-polygalacturonases and pectin lyases that hydrolyze the pectin polymer matrix, allowing mucilage wash-off.
2. **Acidification & pH Trajectory:**
   - Rapid lactic acid production by *Leuconostoc* and *Lactococcus* drops the ferment medium pH from ~5.5–6.0 down to **pH 3.8–4.2**.
   - This drop is essential in real-world processing to suppress enteropathogenic, putrefactive bacteria (*Enterobacteriaceae*) and ochratoxigenic molds (*Aspergillus ochraceus*).
3. **Controlled Inoculation Potential:**
   - The paper demonstrates that indigenous Thai wild strains (*Hanseniaspora uvarum*, *Pichia fermentans*, and *Leuconostoc*) can be developed as customized commercial starter cultures to reproducibly create floral/fruity cup profiles in Southern Robusta, elevating ordinary commercial coffee to Fine/Specialty grades.
